About VESPER

Cinematic Companion & Release Intelligence

VESPER tracks theatrical, digital, VOD, and physical media releases — delivering automated countdown alerts straight to your devices so you never miss a premiere.

Smart Release Alert Cadence (7, 3, & 1 Day)

Our automated background engine monitors global digital and premiere calendars for titles in your watchlist, sending countdown reminders:

7 Days Out
Early warning to get ready for next week’s drop.
3 Days Out
Mid-week reminder as release day approaches.
1 Day & Drop
Premiere day alert with instant streaming links.

Custom Watch Reminders & Calendar Sync

Schedule exact watch dates and times for any movie or TV series. Use 1-tap presets (Tonight, Tomorrow, Weekend Movie Night), sync with Google Calendar, and receive high-priority push notifications when it's time to stream.

Next Episode & Season Air Date Tracking

For ongoing TV series in your watchlist, VESPER automatically tracks the exact air dates, episode titles, and season premiere drops so you never miss what’s next.

Push Notifications via ntfy.sh

Subscribe to your private topic in the free ntfy app (iOS / Android / Desktop) for instant push notifications with rich tags, alert sounds, and direct action links.

Instant Free Streaming on HydraHD

Enjoy 1-click access to stream free HD movies and TV series directly on HydraHD without wading through ads or extra steps.
